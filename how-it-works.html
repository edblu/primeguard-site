The process

How it works

No guessing. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you reach out to the moment the job is done.

1

You tell us what you need

Pick your service, answer a few quick questions, describe the job in your own words, and add photos if you have them. Takes about two minutes. You get an instant ballpark price on the spot.

2

We review it

Our team looks at everything you sent. If we can price it accurately from your details and photos, we will. Simple jobs often get a firm quote right away.

3

A pro evaluates, when needed

If we cannot quote it from your photos alone, we send a vetted local pro to look at the property in person. Free evaluation. No obligation.

4

You get the final quote

The pro submits their quote, we review it, then send it to you. The final price is confirmed before anything starts. No surprises, ever.

5

You approve. We do the job.

Like the quote? Approve it and we schedule the work. The crew shows up, gets it done, and sends before and after photos. You confirm you are happy, then you pay. Not happy? We come back and make it right.

Our promise: you never pay for a surprise. The price is confirmed before work begins, the work is photo-verified when it ends, and our 48-hour make-it-right guarantee covers every job.
