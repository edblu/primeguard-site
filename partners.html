How it works

The fine print, up front

House rules

  • Paid only on collected money. Your share follows the customer's payments: if a payment is refunded, that share reverses on the next statement, and when a customer cancels, the share stops.
  • Partners never quote prices, discounts, or timelines. All pricing is PrimeGuard's.
  • Customers belong to PrimeGuard. No side deals.
  • A W-9 goes on file before your first payout.
  • No spam, no fake reviews, no misleading claims. One violation ends the partnership.
