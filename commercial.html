Questions

Before you ask

What does it cost?

Priced after the free property walk. Every property has a different scope, and the written assessment with photos comes first. We do not publish commercial price lists, and neither does any serious commercial grounds company.

Who approves extra work?

Only the approver you name in the contract, plus a named second for storm emergencies. That way an irrigation repair or a cleanup never waits, and never happens without authority.

How are visits documented?

Checklist and photos on every visit, and a monthly summary written so a manager can forward it to a board or an owner as-is.

What areas do you serve?

Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach Counties, including portfolio properties that need the same scope and invoice format at every site.

Is there a minimum property size?

No. If the grounds matter to your residents, tenants or customers, they are worth a walk.

What happens after a storm?

Storm cleanup is a named service family in the contract, approved by your named approver or emergency second, and documented with the same photo proof as every other visit.
