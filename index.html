Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to be home during the service?

No. As long as we have access to the area, gates, and an outdoor water spigot if needed, we handle everything and send before & after photos.

How fast can you come out?

Most jobs are scheduled within 48 hours. Need it sooner? Call us and we will do our best to fit you in.

What happens if it rains?

We watch the forecast and reschedule outdoor work at no charge. You always know before we roll a truck.

How do I pay?

After the job is done and you are happy. We accept cards and cash — no deposits for most services.

Are your pros vetted?

Yes. Every tech is background-checked and trained on our checklist before touching your property.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Tell us within 48 hours and we will come back and make it right — free. That is our promise.

Who we are

A higher standard of property care

PrimeGuard Preservation is a Miami-Dade property services company built on a simple idea: the work should be excellent, the price should be clear, and you should never have to wonder what happened while you were away.

Every visit is handled by a vetted professional and documented with before-and-after photos, so you see exactly what your money bought. Homeowners, landlords, and property managers across the county trust us with the details that keep a property looking sharp.
