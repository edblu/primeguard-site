Who we are

A higher standard of property care

PrimeGuard Preservation is a Miami-Dade property services company built on a simple idea: the work should be excellent, the price should be clear, and you should never have to wonder what happened while you were away.

Every visit is handled by a vetted professional and documented with before-and-after photos, so you see exactly what your money bought. Homeowners, landlords, and property managers across the county trust us with the details that keep a property looking sharp.
