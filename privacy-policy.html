Privacy Policy

PrimeGuard Preservation LLC. Last updated October 21, 2026.

PrimeGuard Preservation LLC ("PrimeGuard," "we," "us") provides lawn care and property services in Miami-Dade and Broward Counties, Florida, through primeguardmia.com and our phone line, (786) 422-9674. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and service address.
  • Service details: the work you request, appointment times, notes about your property, and photos of your property taken before and after a job or shared by you.
  • Booking and quote records: estimates, signed quotes, payment status, and financing applications handled by third-party financing providers.
  • Communications: calls, texts, and messages with our team, including consent choices you make (for example, whether you agree to receive offers by email or text).

How we use your information

  • To schedule, perform, and document your services, including appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • To send quotes, follow up on quotes you requested, and complete payments.
  • To send job updates and before and after photos of your own property.
  • To ask for a review after a completed job.
  • To send offers and seasonal specials by email or text only when you have given us permission.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send appointment confirmations and reminders, service updates, quote follow-ups, and, with your separate permission, offers. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the service: with the crew member assigned to your job (your name, address, and job details), and with trusted providers that handle scheduling, payments, financing, phone, and messaging for us under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information when required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Click unsubscribe in any marketing email, or tell us and we will remove you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information by contacting us below.
